# 10 · Monolith — "Drag the sun across a house"
Brand: **Monolith** — a small architecture practice (Lisbon/Oslo) known for houses on extreme sites.

## Signature interaction
**The sun is a handle.** The cliff villa diorama (`models/monolith/house.glb`) sits on a plinth in a gallery void (or a
real sky — decide what looks better). Drag the sun along a visible arc (or scroll) through a day: dawn (low, pink) → noon
(hard, white, short shadows) → golden hour (long, amber) → blue hour → night (moonlight, stars, windows glow warm, pool
glows turquoise). Directional light direction/colour/intensity, sky, fog, env intensity and exposure interpolate.
High-quality shadows (tight shadow camera, 4096 map, tuned bias) sweeping across the concrete are the star. A time readout
(06:12, 12:00, 19:41…) and sun azimuth/altitude. Night: emissive window planes placed on the model's glass (inspect the
model, place thin boxes) and a warm point light inside.

## Art direction
Swiss-minimal: warm off-white `#ecebe6`, concrete grey, black type, one accent (sun amber). Fonts: "Inter Tight" +
"IBM Plex Serif" italic. Whitespace, strict 12-col grid, big numerals.

## Must be true
Golden-hour frame: long raking shadows across the house and cliff; night frame: glowing windows and stars.
