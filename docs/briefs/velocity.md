# 07 · Velocity Lab — "See through the shoe"
Brand: **Velocity Lab** — launch page for the VX-1 Carbon, a carbon-plated racing trainer.

## Signature interaction
**The X-ray lens.** A circular lens follows the cursor. Outside the lens the shoe (`models/velocity/white.glb`) renders as a
premium product shot; inside it renders in an alternate mode. Render the scene twice (normal + alternate materials) into
render targets and composite with a circular mask with a refractive rim (slight distortion, chromatic fringe, thin bright ring).
Modes (click or keys 1–3, label on the lens): X-RAY (fresnel-glow translucent shell + procedural internals: the carbon plate
as a curved glowing sheet, foam cells as a noise lattice), THERMAL (heat-map palette from a pressure field on the sole),
CAD (clean edges + measurements). Scroll rotates and repositions the shoe through tech callouts (leader lines from 3D anchor
points to labels). Colourway switch white ↔ black (`black.glb`) via a noise dissolve with a hot glowing edge.

## Page sections
Nav · Hero ("VX-1 Carbon. 186 grams of unfair advantage.") · Lens exploration · Tech (Plate / Foam / Upper, each a camera move
+ stats: 38 mm stack, 8 mm drop, 186 g) · Colourways · Drop countdown with live timer + CTA · Footer.

## Art direction
Clinical lab white `#f2f3f5`, graphite, volt/orange accents; precise grid, measurement ticks, monospace captions.
Fonts: "Archivo" (expanded display) + "JetBrains Mono". Studio floor with soft planar reflection.
