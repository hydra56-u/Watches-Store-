# 20 · Aurel Horologie — "Take a movement apart"
Brand: **Aurel Horologie** — an independent Genevan watchmaker; the calibre AH-01 skeleton tourbillon.

## Signature interaction
Hero: the Meshy watch (`models/horologie/watch.glb`) floating; the cursor tilts it; studio light glints on rose gold.
Then **scroll takes the movement apart**: the watch turns and the camera moves into an exploded view of a **fully procedural
movement** stacked along the axis: mainplate, barrel with mainspring, going train (centre, third, fourth, escape wheels with real
tooth profiles via extruded Shapes), pallet fork, balance wheel with hairspring (oscillating at 4 Hz, 8 beats/s), bridges with
Côtes de Genève stripes (procedural), ruby jewels (transmission). Every gear rotates at the correct ratio and meshes with its
neighbour. Layers separate with labels (leader lines to 3D anchors: "Barrel · 72 h reserve", "Escapement · 28,800 vph").
**Click and hold the crown to wind**: the mainspring tightens, a power-reserve indicator fills, the train speeds up briefly.
Scroll back reassembles.

## Art direction
Ivory `#f3efe7`, navy, rose gold `#c89b6d`. Fonts: "Cormorant Garamond" + "Montserrat" small caps. Light mode, precise, editorial.
Studio env with long softboxes so metals look expensive.

## Must be true
Exploded frame: a floating stack of gleaming gears and bridges with labels, crisp like a technical render.
If the Meshy watch looks poor up close, keep it small/far or replace the hero with the procedural assembled movement.
